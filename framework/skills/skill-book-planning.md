# Skill: Book Planning

This skill helps you organize and plan your book project, including structure, outline, and timeline.

---

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when you need help with:
- Creating book structure and chapter outline
- Determining chapter count and progression
- Planning chapter-by-chapter content
- Creating writing timeline and targets
- Organizing research and resources
- Establishing quality standards

---

## What This Skill Does

### 1. Analyzes Your Book Concept
- Confirms book topic, audience, and purpose
- Identifies themes and main ideas
- Determines book length and scope
- Maps progression from beginning to end

### 2. Creates Detailed Outline
- Chapter-by-chapter breakdown
- Main points and sections per chapter
- Research and resources needed
- Examples and illustrations to include

### 3. Establishes Writing Targets
- Ideal chapter length
- Writing timeline
- Daily/weekly targets
- Quality criteria per chapter

### 4. Prepares You for Writing
- Organizes repository structure
- Gathers research materials
- Creates writing checklists
- Sets up writing discipline and schedule

---

## How to Use This Skill

### Step 1: Provide Your Book Concept
Share:
- What your book is about
- Who will read it
- What they'll gain
- How many chapters you're thinking

### Step 2: Provide Sample Content
Share:
- Book outline or structure (if you have one)
- Chapter topics or themes
- Any sections you've already drafted
- Research materials or data

### Step 3: Answer Planning Questions
I may ask:
- How should chapters be organized?
- Should it be story-driven or concept-driven?
- Any specific challenges or constraints?
- What's your ideal writing timeline?

### Step 4: Review the Plan
I'll provide:
- Detailed chapter outline
- Writing timeline
- Chapter length targets
- Quality criteria
- Resources needed

## Example Planning Session

**You say**: "I want to write a book about productivity for busy professionals. My audience is office workers 25-45. I have a few ideas about time management and focus."

**I ask**: 
1. Do you have any existing drafts or research?
2. Should it be practical how-to or narrative/story-based?
3. How many chapters are you imagining?
4. Any specific techniques or strategies you want to cover?

**You provide**: 
- I have 3 existing sections about time blocking
- Practical how-to approach with real examples
- Thinking 12 chapters
- Want to cover: Planning, Time Blocking, Focus Techniques, Delegation, Tools, Life Integration

**I create**:
- Complete chapter outline (12 chapters + intro + conclusion)
- Section breakdown for each chapter
- Writing timeline (8 weeks for first draft)
- 4,000-5,000 word target per chapter
- Quality checklist for each chapter
- Research and resources needed

When you use this skill, you'll receive:

- Clear topic/audience/purpose summary

- Research to gather
- Resources to prepare

---

## Tips for Effective Planning

1. **Be specific about your book**: The more detail you provide, the better the plan
2. **Think about your reader**: Who are they? What do they want?
3. **Consider your natural structure**: How does content flow naturally?
4. **Don't over-plan**: 3-5 points per chapter is enough; too much detail is constraining
5. **Leave room for discovery**: You may find new ideas while writing
