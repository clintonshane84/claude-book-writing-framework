# Skill: Book Reviewing

This skill helps you edit chapters for quality, clarity, consistency, and readiness for publication.

---

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when you need help with:
- Comprehensive chapter review and editing
- Evaluating chapter quality and completeness
- Identifying areas for improvement
- Ensuring consistency across chapters
- Getting feedback before final publication
- Major revisions or restructuring

---

## What This Skill Does

### 1. Comprehensive Chapter Review
- Evaluates content, structure, clarity, and style
- Provides detailed feedback on strengths and weaknesses
- Identifies specific areas for improvement
- Suggests revisions and enhancements

### 2. Quality Assessment
- Checks that chapter fulfills its purpose
- Verifies all outline points are covered
- Assesses example quality and relevance
- Evaluates overall readability

### 3. Consistency Checking
- Compares to established persona and voice
- Checks terminology consistency
- Verifies chapter connections
- Ensures style matches other chapters

### 4. Constructive Feedback
- Provides specific, actionable feedback
- Suggests improvements, not just criticism
- Maintains respect for your voice and vision
- Balances encouragement with honest assessment

---

## How to Use This Skill

### To Review a Chapter

**Provide**:
- Chapter content (chapter.md file)
- Chapter number and title
- Chapter outline (context for purpose)
- persona-writing-style.md (your established voice)
- Previous chapter (for consistency check)

**I'll**:
- Read chapter completely
- Evaluate content, structure, clarity, voice
- Identify strengths
- Note areas for improvement
- Provide specific suggestions

**Then**:
- Review my feedback
- Implement suggestions you agree with
- Discuss any disagreements
- Revise chapter accordingly

### To Get Specific Feedback

**Ask about**:
- "Is this chapter clear enough for someone new to the topic?"
- "Does this match my established voice?"
- "Are there enough examples?"
- "Does this flow well into the next chapter?"
- "What's the weakest part of this chapter?"

**I'll**:
- Provide specific feedback on your question
- Give examples of improvements
- Explain reasoning
- Offer alternatives if applicable

## Review Dimensions

### Content Review
**Assesses**:
- Does chapter accomplish its purpose?
- Are all outline points covered?
- Is content accurate and appropriate?
- Are examples strong and relevant?
- Is anything missing or unnecessary?

**Questions I ask**:
- What is this chapter trying to do?
- Does it succeed at that purpose?
- What would strengthen the content?

### Structure Review
**Assesses**:
- Does the chapter flow logically?
- Are sections organized well?
- Are transitions smooth?
- Is the opening engaging?
- Is the closing effective?

**Questions I ask**:
- Can I follow the progression of ideas?
- Are there any confusing jumps?
- Does the opening hook?
- Does the closing land well?

### Clarity Review
**Assesses**:
- Is the writing clear and understandable?
- Are sentences well-constructed?
- Is vocabulary appropriate?
- Are there confusing sections?
- Would a new reader understand?

**Questions I ask**:
- Where did I get confused?
- What sections need simplification?
- Are there unclear pronouns or references?

### Voice and Style Review
**Assesses**:
- Does this sound like you?
- Is tone consistent with other chapters?
- Is voice authentic?
- Are there style inconsistencies?
- Does it match persona-writing-style.md?

**Questions I ask**:
- Is this in your voice, or does it sound off?
- How does this compare to previous chapters?
- Are there inauthentic sections?

## Feedback Format

When reviewing, I'll provide:

**Summary**:
- Overall impression and main strengths
- Primary areas for improvement

**Detailed Feedback**:
- Section-by-section notes
- Specific observations
- Suggested improvements

**Strengths**:
- What's working well
- Strong sections
- Effective elements

**Areas for Improvement**:
- Clarity issues
- Structure problems
- Missing content
- Voice inconsistencies

**Specific Suggestions**:
- How to address each issue
- Example rewrites if helpful
- Alternative approaches

---

## Revision After Review

**Process**:
1. Read through my feedback carefully
2. Understand the concerns
3. Decide which feedback to implement
4. Make revisions to chapter
5. Re-read revised chapter
6. Commit to git with revision message

**Remember**:
- You're the author; final decisions are yours
- Not all feedback needs to be implemented
- Trust your instincts about your voice
- But consider feedback seriously

---

## Common Review Findings

**Too much summary, not enough examples**:
- Add specific stories or case studies
- Replace abstract explanation with concrete illustration
- Show, don't just tell

**Voice sounds different from other chapters**:
- Reread persona-writing-style.md
- Adjust sentence structure or vocabulary
- Compare opening to previous chapter
- Bring tone back into alignment

**Structure is confusing**:
- Reorder sections for better flow
- Add transition sentences
- Clarify how each section relates
- Strengthen connections between ideas

**Not enough depth**:
- Expand explanations
- Add more examples
- Provide supporting details
- Develop ideas more fully

**Unclear or confusing sections**:
- Simplify complex sentences
- Clarify pronoun references
- Explain technical terms
- Reorganize for clarity

---

## Quality Standards

A chapter is ready for publication when it:
- ✅ Accomplishes its stated purpose
- ✅ Has clear, easy-to-follow structure
- ✅ Uses authentic, consistent voice
- ✅ Includes relevant examples
- ✅ Flows smoothly from start to finish
- ✅ Connects well with surrounding chapters
- ✅ Is free of major clarity issues
- ✅ Matches quality of other chapters
