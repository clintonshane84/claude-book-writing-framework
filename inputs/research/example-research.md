## Study: "The Impact of Planning on Project Success" (2024)

**Source**: Journal of Project Management, Vol. 45, No. 2  
**Authors**: Smith, J., Johnson, M., & Williams, K.  
**Date**: March 2024  
**Link**: https://example.com/research/planning-study-2024

### Key Findings

1. **Planning Reduces Failure Rate by 60%**
   - Projects with written plans succeed 78% of the time
   - Projects without plans succeed only 32% of the time
   - This holds true across industries and team sizes

2. **First 24 Hours Critical**
   - Planning completed in first day of project launch improves outcomes by 45%
   - Delays in planning directly correlate to timeline overruns
   - Quality of initial plan directly impacts final results

3. **Team Alignment**
   - Teams with documented, shared plans communicate 3x more effectively
   - Conflicts reduced when everyone understands the roadmap
   - Buy-in increases when team members contribute to planning

### Statistics to Use

- 78% success rate for planned projects
- 32% success rate for unplanned projects
- 45% improvement when planning happens in first 24 hours
- 3x more effective communication with documented plans
