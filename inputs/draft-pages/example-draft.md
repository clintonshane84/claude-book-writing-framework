This chapter will walk you through the first steps of bringing your project to life, from validating your concept to organizing your materials.

## How to Use This

When you ask Claude to write this chapter, it will:
1. Read this partial draft
2. Use the opening you've written as a foundation
3. Develop the key points you've outlined
4. Add examples and detailed guidance
5. Create smooth transitions
6. Write an engaging conclusion

You don't need to write everything—just provide enough context and examples for Claude to continue in your voice and style.
