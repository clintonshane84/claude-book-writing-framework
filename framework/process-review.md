# Phase 3: Review Process

The Review phase is where you edit, refine, and polish your chapters for clarity, consistency, and quality. This phase transforms rough drafts into polished, publication-ready chapters.

**Duration**: Typically 1-3 weeks depending on book length and depth of revision needed

**Outcome**: Polished, reviewed chapters ready for Phase 4 (Publishing)

---

## Phase 3 Overview

The Review phase focuses on quality and consistency:

1. **Chapter-by-chapter review**: Each chapter receives comprehensive editing
2. **Multi-level editing**: Content, structure, clarity, style, grammar
3. **Consistency checking**: Voice, terminology, references throughout book
4. **Quality assurance**: Final polish before publication

---

## Review Levels

### Level 1: Content Review
**Focus**: Does the chapter accomplish its purpose?

**Questions**:
- Does this chapter fulfill its intended purpose?
- Are all main points from outline covered?
- Is anything missing or unnecessary?
- Do examples and illustrations support the content?
- Is technical accuracy verified?

**Review checklist**:
- [ ] Chapter purpose is clear
- [ ] All outline points are covered
- [ ] Examples are relevant and illustrative
- [ ] No major content gaps
- [ ] Technical accuracy verified

**Fix if needed**:
- Add missing content from outline
- Remove irrelevant sections
- Strengthen weak examples
- Verify facts and data accuracy
- Reorganize for better flow

### Level 2: Structure and Flow Review
**Focus**: Is the chapter well-organized and easy to follow?

**Questions**:
- Do sections flow logically?
- Is the progression clear from beginning to end?
- Are there smooth transitions between ideas?
- Is the chapter length appropriate?
- Does each paragraph support the main idea?

**Review checklist**:
- [ ] Opening clearly introduces topic
- [ ] Sections flow in logical order
- [ ] Transitions between sections are smooth
- [ ] Closing summarizes and bridges forward
- [ ] Paragraph topics are clear
- [ ] Chapter length is appropriate

**Fix if needed**:
- Reorder sections for better flow
- Add transition sentences
- Clarify paragraph topics
- Remove redundant content
- Strengthen opening or closing

### Level 3: Clarity and Readability Review
**Focus**: Can readers easily understand what's written?

**Questions**:
- Would a first-time reader understand this?
- Are sentences clear and well-constructed?
- Is vocabulary appropriate for audience?
- Are paragraphs well-developed?
- Is there unnecessary jargon or complexity?

**Review checklist**:
- [ ] No confusing or unclear sentences
- [ ] Vocabulary matches audience level
- [ ] Jargon is explained when used
- [ ] Paragraphs have clear main ideas
- [ ] Sentence length varies for readability
- [ ] Pronouns clearly refer to their nouns

**Fix if needed**:
- Simplify overly complex sentences
- Explain or define technical terms
- Vary sentence length
- Strengthen paragraph topics
- Clarify pronoun references

### Level 4: Style and Voice Review
**Focus**: Does this sound like you? Is it consistent?

**Questions**:
- Does this chapter match established persona?
- Is tone consistent with other chapters?
- Are vocabulary and phrasing characteristic of your voice?
- Does it sound authentic and genuine?
- Are any stylistic inconsistencies apparent?

**Review checklist**:
- [ ] Tone matches persona-writing-style.md
- [ ] Vocabulary consistent with established voice
- [ ] Narrative approach matches other chapters
- [ ] No awkward or inauthentic phrasing
- [ ] Consistency with previous chapters

**Fix if needed**:
- Adjust tone to match persona
- Replace inauthentic phrases
- Ensure vocabulary consistency
- Strengthen authentic voice
- Compare to and match previous chapters

### Level 5: Grammar and Polish Review
**Focus**: Does it read smoothly with correct mechanics?

**Questions**:
- Are there grammar errors?
- Is punctuation correct and effective?
- Is spelling correct?
- Are sentences well-constructed?
- Does it flow smoothly when read aloud?

**Review checklist**:
- [ ] No grammar errors
- [ ] Punctuation is correct
- [ ] Spelling is correct
- [ ] Active voice used appropriately
- [ ] Sentence construction is sound
- [ ] Reads smoothly when read aloud

**Fix if needed**:
- Correct grammar errors
- Fix punctuation issues
- Correct spelling
- Strengthen weak sentence constructions
- Polish repetitive phrasing

## Chapter Review Workflow

For each chapter, follow this workflow:

### Step 1: Prepare for Review
Before reviewing, set yourself up for success:

**Preparation**:
- [ ] Take a break since writing (ideally a few days)
- [ ] Clear your mind; approach with fresh perspective
- [ ] Have persona-writing-style.md available
- [ ] Have writing-rules.md for reference
- [ ] Previous/next chapters available for context
- [ ] Use distraction-free reading environment

**Duration**: 5-10 minutes

### Step 2: Level 1 Content Review
Read for content and completeness:

**Process**:
1. Read through chapter without stopping
2. Note any missing content
3. Mark sections that seem out of place
4. Check facts and data accuracy
5. Assess example quality

**Make notes on**:
- Content gaps or inconsistencies
- Examples that need strengthening
- Sections to reorganize
- Facts to verify

**Duration**: 20-30 minutes

### Step 3: Level 2 Structure Review
Read for flow and organization:

**Process**:
1. Read opening: Does it hook and introduce?
2. Read transitions: Do sections flow logically?
3. Read closing: Does it summarize and bridge forward?
4. Identify any sections that feel out of place
5. Check paragraph progression

**Make notes on**:
- Sections that should be reordered
- Weak transitions
- Unclear paragraph topics
- Missing connections between ideas

**Duration**: 15-20 minutes

### Step 4: Level 3 Clarity Review
Read for ease of understanding:

**Process**:
1. Read each sentence carefully
2. Flag unclear or confusing sentences
3. Mark jargon that needs explanation
4. Check for overly complex language
5. Look for unclear pronouns

**Try this technique**:
- Read aloud to catch awkward phrasing
- Imagine a new reader; would they understand?
- Look for sentences longer than 25 words
- Flag passive voice constructions

**Make notes on**:
- Sentences to simplify
- Terms to define
- Pronouns to clarify
- Passive voice to convert

**Duration**: 20-30 minutes

### Step 5: Level 4 Voice Review
Read for style consistency:

**Process**:
1. Reread persona-writing-style.md
2. Compare chapter voice to persona
3. Check consistency with previous chapters
4. Look for inauthentic phrasing
5. Assess whether it sounds like you

**Comparison technique**:
- Read a paragraph from this chapter
- Read a paragraph from previous chapter
- Do they sound like same author?
- Are there tone/vocabulary shifts?

**Make notes on**:
- Places where voice shifts
- Inauthentic phrasing
- Tone inconsistencies
- Words/phrases to adjust

**Duration**: 15-20 minutes

### Step 6: Level 5 Grammar Review
Polish for correctness:

**Process**:
1. Read slowly, word by word
2. Check grammar and punctuation
3. Verify spelling
4. Look for sentence construction issues
5. Read aloud for smooth flow

**Tools to use**:
- Grammarly or similar (if available)
- Manual reading for catches tool misses
- Read aloud to hear awkwardness
- Compare punctuation to writing-rules.md style

**Make notes on**:
- Grammar errors
- Punctuation problems
- Spelling issues
- Awkward sentence construction

**Duration**: 20-30 minutes

### Step 7: Make Revisions
Implement all noted changes:

**Process**:
1. Make content additions/deletions
2. Reorganize sections if needed
3. Strengthen examples
4. Rewrite unclear sentences
5. Adjust tone/voice
6. Fix grammar and spelling

**Strategy**:
- Work through changes systematically
- One type of fix at a time
- Read revised sections to verify improvement
- Don't introduce new issues while fixing old ones

**Duration**: 30-60 minutes (depends on extent of revisions)

### Step 8: Final Read-Through
Do a final review of revised chapter:

**Process**:
1. Read entire revised chapter start to finish
2. Look for any remaining issues
3. Ensure revisions improved without breaking flow
4. Do one final proofread
5. Verify chapter is ready for publication

**Duration**: 15-20 minutes

### Step 9: Commit Revisions
Save and push revised chapter:

**Process**:
1. Save all changes to `chapter.md`
2. Save backup of original to `drafts/draft-v2-reviewed.md`
3. Stage changes: `git add book/NN-chapter-name/`
4. Commit: `git commit -m "Review and revise chapter NN: Chapter Title"`
5. Push to remote: `git push origin main`

**Message examples**:
```
Review chapter 01: Intro - improve clarity and flow
Revise chapter 05: Strategies - strengthen examples
Polish chapter 08: Complete content review and edits
```

**Duration**: 5 minutes

## Consistency Checking: Full Book Review

After reviewing individual chapters, do a full-book review for consistency:

### 1. Terminology Consistency
Check that key terms are used consistently:

**Process**:
- Create list of key terms in book
- Search for each term across all chapters
- Verify usage is consistent
- Correct inconsistencies

**Example**:
```
Key term: "Growth mindset"
Check all chapters:
- Ch. 1: "growth mindset" ✓
- Ch. 2: "growth mentality" ✗ (inconsistent - change to "growth mindset")
- Ch. 3: "growth mindset" ✓
- Ch. 4: "the growth mindset approach" ✓
```

### 2. Reference Consistency
Verify all references to characters, stories, data are consistent:

**Process**:
- Track character/person names
- Verify data/statistics cited consistently
- Check previous chapter references
- Look for inconsistent details

**Example**:
```
Character: Sarah
- Ch. 2: "Sarah's story shows..." ✓
- Ch. 5: "Earlier we met Susan..." ✗ (Should be Sarah)
- Ch. 8: References to Sarah's journey ✓
```

### 3. Format Consistency
Ensure formatting is consistent across chapters:

**Check**:
- Header levels and hierarchy
- Emphasis (bold, italic) usage
- List formatting
- Callout or blockquote styling
- Image captions

### 4. Tone and Voice Consistency
Read chapter openings in sequence:

**Process**:
- Read opening paragraph of each chapter
- Do they sound like same author?
- Are there unexpected tone shifts?
- Revise any chapters with inconsistent voice

### 5. Chapter Length Consistency
Verify chapters are roughly consistent in length:

**Process**:
- Check word count or page count per chapter
- Identify any outliers (unusually short/long)
- Determine if variation makes sense
- Adjust if needed for consistency

## Managing Revisions

### Revision Depth
Different chapters may need different depths of revision:

**Light review**: Content is solid, just polish for clarity and grammar
- Time: 30-45 minutes per chapter
- Changes: Minor clarity fixes, grammar corrections

**Standard review**: Content is good but needs reorganization and clarification
- Time: 60-90 minutes per chapter
- Changes: Reorganize, strengthen, clarify

**Deep review**: Content needs significant work
- Time: 2-3 hours per chapter
- Changes: Major reorganization, significant rewriting

### Iteration Cycles
Some chapters may need multiple review passes:

**Example cycle**:
1. First pass: Content and structure review
2. Second pass: Clarity and voice review
3. Third pass: Grammar and polish
4. Final pass: Quick verify revised chapter works

### Keeping Track of Changes
Use comments or tracking if needed:

**In chapter.md**:
- `[REVISED]` marks major changes
- `[CLARIFIED]` marks clarity improvements
- `[RESTRUCTURED]` marks reordered sections
- Remove markers before final publication

## Common Review Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ **Over-editing**: Some rough edges are okay; don't perfect forever
- ❌ **Losing author voice**: Edit for clarity, not to change voice
- ❌ **Ignoring content gaps**: Address missing content, don't just polish surface
- ❌ **No fresh perspective**: Take breaks between writing and reviewing
- ❌ **Inconsistent standards**: Apply same review standards to every chapter
- ❌ **Forgetting context**: Remember full book purpose and audience
- ❌ **Perfectionism paralysis**: "Done" is better than "perfect"

## Signs Review Phase is Complete

✅ **Review phase is complete when**:
- All chapters have been through 5-level review
- Revisions have been made and verified
- Full-book consistency has been checked
- Terminology, references, and voice are consistent
- Grammar and spelling are correct
- All changes are committed and pushed
- Chapters are publication-ready
- You're ready to move to Phase 4 (Publishing)
