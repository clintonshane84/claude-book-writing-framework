# Skill: Book Research

This skill helps you find, organize, and incorporate research materials that support your book's content.

---

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when you need help with:
- Finding research materials and sources
- Organizing research by topic
- Extracting relevant information
- Integrating research into chapters
- Managing citations and bibliography
- Verifying claims with sources

---

## What This Skill Does

### 1. Identifies Research Needs
- Understands what information your book needs
- Identifies gaps in your knowledge
- Suggests types of sources to look for
- Prioritizes research by chapter

### 2. Suggests Research Directions
- Recommends types of sources (books, studies, interviews, etc.)
- Suggests specific topics to research
- Identifies experts or authorities
- Points to databases or resources

### 3. Organizes Research Materials
- Creates research summary documents
- Organizes information by chapter
- Extracts key facts and quotes
- Manages source documentation

### 4. Incorporates Research into Writing
- Suggests how to integrate data/statistics
- Recommends where to include sources
- Helps incorporate quotes appropriately
- Maintains proper attribution

## Research Organization

**Create research structure**:
```
/resources/research/
├── research-master.md (all sources listed)
├── research-by-chapter/
│   ├── chapter-01-notes.md
│   ├── chapter-02-notes.md
│   └── [continuing chapters]
└── sources/
    ├── books.md
    ├── articles.md
    ├── websites.md
    └── interviews.md
```

**Each research file should include**:
- Topic/chapter it relates to
- Key information extracted
- Quotes or relevant passages
- Source citation
- Page numbers or links
- How it will be used

## Incorporating Research into Writing

**Best practices**:
- Integrate naturally, don't force
- Support points with evidence
- Cite sources appropriately
- Use quotes sparingly and effectively
- Explain data and statistics
- Connect research to your argument

**Example**:
```
Research shows that 78% of professionals struggle with time management 
(Johnson Study, 2023). In my own experience, this comes down to one core issue: 
we often confuse urgency with importance. Once you understand this distinction, 
managing time becomes much more strategic.
```

## Creating Bibliography

Keep track of all sources for bibliography:

**Format**:
```markdown
# Bibliography

## Books
- Author. "Title." Publisher, Year.

## Studies and Papers
- Author. "Study Title." Journal, Year.

## Websites
- Website Name. URL. Accessed: Date.

## Interviews
- Name, Date (personal communication/interview)
```

## Managing Sources Ethically

**Always**:
- Cite sources appropriately
- Distinguish between research findings and your opinion
- Quote accurately
- Get permission for interviews
- Respect intellectual property

**Avoid**:
- Plagiarism
- Misquoting sources
- Presenting others' ideas as your own
- Using copyrighted material without permission

## Research Timeline

**Typical research flow**:
1. During planning: Identify what research is needed
2. Before writing: Gather key sources for chapter
3. While writing: Reference research as needed
4. During review: Verify citations and accuracy
5. Before publishing: Create final bibliography

---

## Common Research Challenges

**Too much research material**:
- Be selective; use only most relevant
- Organize by chapter
- Focus on sources that directly support your arguments

**Hard to find information**:
- Try different search terms
- Look in related fields
- Consider if you have enough without this specific information
- Suggest different approach or argument

**Need recent data**:
- Check recent studies and reports
- Look for 2022-2024 sources if possible
- Note if data is historical
- Consider gathering your own data (survey, etc.)

**Copyright concerns**:
- Use proper citations
- Don't reproduce entire articles or chapters
- Use quotes sparingly and attribute
- Paraphrase and cite when using ideas
